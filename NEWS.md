# sdvmodels 0.1.0

* Initial release: the college football models of the `cfb_model_artifacts`
  release (expected points, win probability with and without the spread,
  field goal, fourth down conversion, two point conversion, expected pass,
  completion probability and quarterback rating) as raw `xgboost` vectors,
  plus the punt distance distribution, the field position expected points
  table, the model cards and the bundle manifest. Bundle version 2026.09.09.
